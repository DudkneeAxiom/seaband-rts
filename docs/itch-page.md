# Commodore — itch.io page copy

Jam theme: **Charge**. Everything below is written to be pasted straight into
the itch page fields. Nothing here is generated at runtime; it exists so the
submission and the build tell the same story.

---

## Short blurb (the one-liner under the title)

> Take charge of a cutter, a crew and a debt you did not run up.

A commodore is what a captain becomes the moment a second ship answers to him.
That is the whole game: one battered hull, and an account to settle.

---

## Page description

**You inherit the *Marlin's Debt*.** A tired cutter, a handful of hands who
have not been paid yet, and an ocean of other people's sails. The debt is not
yours. The hull, the crew and every charge against her are, from this morning
on.

Trade, escort, hunt, or take what you need off somebody else's deck. Every prize
you keep is a second hull under your flag, and a fleet is something you give
orders to: **FOLLOW**, **CHARGE**, **HOLD**, hold fire. Close the last two
hundred metres yourself, lay her alongside, and charge the rail.

Six chapters, six powers with water of their own, and an account that ends one
of two ways.

### How it answers the theme

*Charge* is three things here, and the game was built on all of them before the
theme was announced:

- **The charges against you.** You start owing. The last chapter is called
  *Paid in Full*.
- **Taking charge.** One ship becomes a squadron, and the fleet bar is where
  you command it.
- **The charge.** Guns are cold on the open sea — nobody fires at range. An
  action begins when you *close*, and boarding is a charge across a rail.

### What the demo shows

One playable slice: the questionnaire that builds your captain, the open sea,
six harbours with towns and people, trade and contracts, chases, gunnery,
boarding, prizes, and a fleet you give orders to. The objective card top-left
always tells you what is **in your charge** next.

### Controls

- **Tap or click the water** to set a course. Tap a ship to mark her; tap again
  — or press **RUN HER DOWN** — to give chase.
- **FIRE** when a side bears. **BOARD** to close and grapple.
- Keys are listed in-game on the **HELM** page.
- Plays in the browser, on a phone in landscape as well as on a desktop.

### Notes

- No engine, no bundler, no network at runtime: plain ES modules and one
  vendored copy of three.js. Everything — terrain, ships, ports, music, every
  sound — is generated in the browser.
- The music is written live rather than looped, so a long passage never
  arrives back where it started.
- Runs entirely offline; the single-file build is one HTML document.
